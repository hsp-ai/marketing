# Marketing Channel Identification

## Objective
The objective of this query is to identify all distinct marketing channels available in the dataset.

## SQL Approach
The `DISTINCT` keyword is used to retrieve unique marketing channel names from the `channel` column, eliminating duplicate values.

## Results
The query identified **7 distinct marketing channels**:

1. Affiliate
2. Email
3. Google Ads
4. LinkedIn
5. Meta Ads
6. Organic
7. TikTok

## Conclusion
The dataset includes seven different marketing channels, covering affiliate marketing, email marketing, paid advertising platforms, professional social networking, organic traffic, and social media marketing.

Identifying these channels provides an overview of the marketing activities represented in the dataset and supports further analysis of performance across channels.
