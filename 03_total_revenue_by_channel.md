# Revenue Analysis by Marketing Channel

## Objective
The objective of this analysis is to calculate the total revenue generated by each marketing channel and compare their revenue contributions.

## SQL Approach
The `SUM()` aggregate function is used to calculate total revenue for each channel. The `GROUP BY` clause groups records by marketing channel, allowing revenue to be aggregated separately for each channel.

## Results

The analysis identified the following total revenue figures by marketing channel

 Marketing Channel  Total Revenue 
------
 LinkedIn  665,081.15 
 Email  509,426.90 
 Organic  426,282.41 
 Affiliate  425,789.82 
 Meta Ads  412,845.02 
 Google Ads  350,750.79 
 TikTok  338,118.51 

## Key Findings
- LinkedIn generated the highest total revenue, at approximately 665,081.15.
- Email ranked second, generating approximately 509,426.90.
- Organic and Affiliate generated similar revenue, at approximately 426,282.41 and 425,789.82, respectively.
- TikTok generated the lowest total revenue among the seven channels, at approximately 338,118.51.

## Conclusion
LinkedIn was the leading channel in terms of total revenue, followed by Email. These results provide an initial comparison of revenue contributions across marketing channels.

However, total revenue alone does not indicate profitability or marketing efficiency. Further analysis should consider advertising spend, conversion volume, return on ad spend (ROAS), and return on investment (ROI) to evaluate channel performance more comprehensively.

Note Revenue values are rounded to two decimal places for readability. The currency is not specified in the provided results.
